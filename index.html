Before You Go

What foreign travelers actually ask

The practical questions that decide whether the trip goes smoothly.

Timing

Best time to visit

October to March — daytime is warm, nights in the desert are cold. April to June is punishing heat.

Entry

Visa on arrival / e-Visa

Most nationalities can apply for an Indian e-Visa online before travel — apply at least a week ahead.

Budget

Daily cost, realistically

Budget travelers manage on $25–35/day. Mid-range comfort with a driver runs closer to $70–100/day.

Safety

Solo & solo female travel

Rajasthan is well-traveled and generally safe in tourist areas — standard precautions apply, same as any country.
