Jaipur — the Pink City, Rajasthan's grand entrance

Forts on hilltops, a walled old city painted terracotta-pink, and bazaars that have sold the same trades for 300 years. This is where most US and UK travelers begin their Rajasthan trip.

Best timeOct – Mar

Who's already coming here

Rajasthan welcomed 20.7 lakh (2.07 million) foreign tourists in 2024 — up 21.9% from the year before. The US and UK aren't a small niche here; they're the two biggest source markets, ahead of France, Germany, and Japan.

In plain terms: if you're American or British, you won't be the only foreigner in Jaipur — you'll be part of the two largest groups, and the city's tourist infrastructure (English signage, card payments at most hotels, US/UK plug adapters sold everywhere) reflects that.

What to see

11 KM OUT · 2–3 HRS · GO BEFORE 9AM

Amber Fort

Hilltop fort-palace over Maota Lake. The Sheesh Mahal's mirror-tiled ceiling turns one candle into a thousand points of light.

OLD CITY · 1.5–2 HRS

City Palace

Still partly home to Jaipur's royal family. Two of the world's largest silver vessels are on display, once used to carry Ganges water abroad.

EDGE OF OLD CITY · 30–45 MIN

Hawa Mahal

A five-story pink facade with 953 windows, built so royal women could watch the street unseen. Best at sunrise from a rooftop café across the road.

NEXT TO CITY PALACE · 45 MIN

Jantar Mantar

An 18th-century observatory, UNESCO-listed, with the world's largest stone sundial — accurate to within two seconds.

Budget, in your currency

Mid-range travelers typically spend $70–100 / £55–80 per day in Jaipur including a 3-star hotel, private driver, and meals. Budget travelers manage on $25–35 / £20–28 per day using local transport and guesthouses. Most hotels and restaurants in tourist areas accept cards, but keep some cash for auto-rickshaws and small vendors.

FAQs

How many days do I need in Jaipur?

Two full days covers the main sites comfortably. Add a third for a slower pace or a day trip to Abhaneri stepwell.

Do US and UK citizens need a visa?

Yes — both need an e-Visa for India, applied for online before travel. Processing usually takes a few business days.

Is Jaipur safe for foreign tourists?

Yes, it's one of Rajasthan's most tourist-friendly cities. Agree on auto-rickshaw fares beforehand and use prepaid taxis from the airport.
